How to remove the Gemini watermark — the 3-step version

The free Gemini watermark remover erases the visible AI badge from your photos and videos without touching anything else. No sign-up, no cloud upload, no quality loss — the whole job runs on your own computer.

  1. Upload — drop your PNG, JPG, WebP, MP4, MOV or WebM into the tool. It never leaves your machine.
  2. Select — drag a tight box over the Gemini watermark. A pixel-exact preview confirms the region before anything is edited.
  3. Remove & download — only those pixels are inpainted with grain-matched texture; everything else stays byte-identical. Photos keep original resolution; video is encoded once at visually lossless quality with the original audio.

Gemini watermark removal — FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — no accounts, no usage limits, and no watermark stamped on your output.

Will my image or video lose quality?

Photos save at original resolution at quality 100. Video is encoded once with x264 at CRF 17 (visually lossless), same resolution, FPS, and original audio. Pixels outside your selection are untouched.

Does it work on videos?

Yes — scrub to a frame where the badge is sharp, drag one box, and the tool cleans every frame of the whole clip in a single pass with no flicker.

Where do my files go?

Nowhere. The engine runs locally on your computer; nothing is uploaded to a cloud service.

Does it remove invisible SynthID?

No. The tool removes the visible badge only — it doesn't add, remove, or alter hidden signatures. Use it on content you own.

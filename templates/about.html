About Gemini Watermark Remover

A small, opinionated tool built around a simple promise: remove the watermark you point at, keep everything else exactly as it was, and never send your media anywhere.

The three promises

  • You decide what's edited. No automatic detection, no "AI magic" touching your whole frame. You draw the box; only those pixels change — and a preview proves the alignment before anything runs.
  • Original quality stays. Photos keep their full resolution. Video is encoded once at visually lossless x264 CRF 17 with original audio — no double compression, no resolution drops.
  • Your files stay yours. The engine runs on your computer. No uploads to a cloud, no accounts, no analytics on your media.

How it's built

Under the hood it's a lightweight local web app: a Python/Flask engine using OpenCV inpainting (Navier–Stokes + Telea fringe pass) with grain matching for natural-looking fills, and FFmpeg's x264 encoder driven in a single streaming pass for video. That's why it's fast — a one-minute 1080p clip usually finishes in seconds to under a minute.

What we won't do

We won't claim to remove invisible SynthID signatures (nobody can do that reliably, and we won't pretend otherwise). We won't lock features behind a paywall. And we won't add watermarks or branding to your cleaned output.

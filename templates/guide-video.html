How to remove the Gemini watermark from videos

Video is the impressive part: you box the badge once, and the tool cleans it across every frame of the whole clip in a single pass — original audio included.

How it works under the hood

The tool samples several frames spread across the clip (start, middle, end, quarters), builds a tight pixel mask of the badge from each, and unions them. That combined mask is applied to every frame — so it stays rock-steady with no flicker, even where the background changes behind the badge. Frames stream directly into the x264 encoder, which means one encode, no intermediate files, and typical speeds around real-time for 1080p clips.

Step-by-step

  1. Load your video

    MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM, MKV or AVI. Use the scrub bar below the frame to move through the clip. Tap the video to play/pause.

  2. Pause on a frame where the badge is sharp

    Pick a moment with good contrast around the watermark — not motion-blurred — so the mask locks onto the badge cleanly.

  3. Drag a box around the badge

    A preview crop from the middle of the video shows exactly what's selected. Because the badge may drift slightly between scenes, box generously: cover the badge in all its positions plus a few pixels of margin.

  4. Press Remove and wait one pass

    Every frame is cleaned and encoded at x264 CRF 17 — visually lossless — at the same resolution and FPS as the source, with the original audio track copied through.

  5. Preview and download

    Check the result in the right panel (tap to play, or hit Maximize for fullscreen), then download the MP4.

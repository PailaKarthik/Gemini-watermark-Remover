How to remove the Gemini watermark in 3 steps

No accounts, no watermarks-on-your-watermark, no cloud uploads. The whole job — from upload to download — happens on your own machine and usually takes under a minute.

  1. Upload your photo or video

    Drag the file into the remover or click the drop zone to browse. PNG, JPG, WebP, MP4, MOV and WebM are supported. Nothing is sent to a server — the file is processed by a small engine running on your own computer.

  2. Drag a box over the Gemini watermark

    The badge sits in a corner — usually bottom-left or bottom-right. Drag a tight selection box around it. You can scrub a video to any frame first, and a preview thumbnail confirms the exact pixels you're about to clean before anything happens.

  3. Press Remove, then download

    Only the pixels inside your box are edited — the rest of the image or every other frame of the video passes through untouched. Photos save at their original resolution; videos are re-encoded once at visually lossless quality with the original audio. Then just hit download.

Why drag-and-select instead of automatic detection?

Automatic "one-click" removers guess where the watermark is — and guess wrong on busy backgrounds, cropped screenshots, or unusual badge positions. When they fail they either leave half a watermark behind or blur a chunk of your actual content.

Manual selection gives you full control: you draw the box exactly where the badge is, see a pixel-exact preview, and can redraw as many times as you like before committing. It's faster in practice because there's no waiting for a detection model — the removal starts the moment you press the button.

Good to know: the remover erases the visible Gemini badge. Google's invisible SynthID metadata may still exist in the file — this tool doesn't add or remove hidden signatures.

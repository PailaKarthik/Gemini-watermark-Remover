SHORT & HONEST

Privacy policy

The short version: your media never leaves your computer. The details, briefly:

1. Your files

Uploaded photos and videos are processed by the engine running on your own machine. They are stored in local uploads and outputs folders on your computer, which you can inspect and empty at any time. No file is transmitted to us or to any third party.

2. What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics on your media, no telemetry, and no advertising cookies. The tool itself is served locally; the only external requests your browser makes are for web fonts (Google Fonts) when online — your images and videos are never part of that traffic.

Job data in memory

While the app runs it keeps a small in-memory state for each job (status, logs, timing) so the interface can show progress. It stays in RAM on your machine and disappears when the app closes.

3. Cookies

The application sets no tracking cookies. None are needed — there's no session, login, or ad system.

4. Children

The tool is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Use it (as with any media tool) under adult supervision where appropriate.

5. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will appear on this page. Given the local-first design, that's unlikely to be about data collection — there isn't any.

How to remove the Gemini watermark from photos

The Gemini badge on AI-generated images sits in the lower area of the frame. Here's how to erase it cleanly — at original resolution, without blurring your image, free.

What you need

  • The photo (PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP or TIFF) on your computer
  • The free Gemini Watermark Remover — no install beyond the local app, no account

Step-by-step

  1. Open the tool and drop your photo

    Drag the image onto the drop zone or click to browse. It appears instantly in the left panel — it never leaves your machine.

  2. Drag a tight box over the badge

    Zoom mentally into the watermark and draw a box that hugs it — slightly larger than the badge itself, but not a huge rectangle. A pixel-exact preview appears so you can verify you've got the right spot.

  3. Check the preview, then press Remove

    Wrong spot? Just drag again — the preview updates every time. When it's right, press Remove. The tool builds a pixel-tight mask, inpaints the region, and matches the surrounding grain.

  4. Download at original resolution

    The cleaned image saves at the same pixel dimensions as the original, quality 100. Only the pixels inside your box differ from the source.

Tips for the cleanest result

  • Box tight, not huge. A snug box means the inpainting has more surrounding detail to learn from, and less area to fill.
  • Include the full badge plus 2–4px of margin. Cutting it too close can leave a faint outline; too wide wastes background.
  • Busy backgrounds are fine. The fill follows local texture — grass, skin, fabric and gradients all reconstruct believably.
  • Flat backgrounds are easiest. On solid or gradient areas the fill is essentially invisible.
Note: this removes the visible badge only. Invisible SynthID signatures may remain in the file.

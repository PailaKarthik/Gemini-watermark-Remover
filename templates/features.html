EVERYTHING THIS TOOL DOES

Features that make this watermark remover different

Most removers blur a rectangle and hope you won't notice. This one was built around three rules: edit only what you select, keep the output at original quality, and never let a single byte leave your machine.

◎

Pixel-exact manual selection

You drag the box, so you decide exactly which pixels get touched. A live preview shows the cropped region before removal — no surprises.

⬚

Region-only editing

Everything outside your selection is byte-identical to the original. No global sharpening, no full-frame re-compression artifacts, no hidden "enhancement".

✦

Texture-aware inpainting

A tight pixel mask is built inside your box, then filled with Navier–Stokes inpainting plus a Telea fringe pass — the fill follows the surrounding texture.

◍

Grain matching

The cleaned patch is matched to the local noise level of the surrounding image, so the removed area doesn't look like a suspiciously smooth rectangle.

▣

Original-quality output

Photos save at their full original resolution. Video is encoded once with x264 at CRF 17 (visually lossless), same resolution and FPS, original audio track kept.

⚡

Fast single-pass video pipeline

Cleaned frames stream straight into the encoder — no intermediate files, no double transcode. A one-minute 1080p clip typically finishes in well under a minute.

⌂

100% local & private

The engine runs on your computer. Your media is never uploaded to a cloud service, never stored on someone else's server, and never used for training.

✓

Free & unlimited

No accounts, no subscriptions, no export limits, no watermark-on-your-output. Open the page and use it as many times as you want.
